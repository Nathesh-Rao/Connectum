# Record actions: Send, Update status, and the organisation's history

Replaces the old ticket and task flow (the Status field type, its actions table, `status.*`), which is gone. Nothing was migrated: it starts fresh. Written 11 Nov 2026.

## In simple words

- Every record has a **holder**: the person it is with now. It starts with whoever made it (the **initiator**).
- **Send** hands the record to someone else. Whoever last sent it to the current holder is the **assignor**.
- **Update status** moves the record to another status (with comments and an optional completion %). It shows only for a form that has a status. In a form that has a closing (final) status, a closing status always sets the completion to 100 (see **Completion and closing**).
- The actions are in the **three-dot menu of the record's card** in chat (where Forward and React are). **Send shows only for a form that switches it on** (Studio, "Send" > **Enable send**); Update status shows only for a form that has a status. The Inbox, Smart View and My submissions show the same actions as buttons.
- **Auto send** (Studio, "Auto send" > **Enable auto send**): every new record is sent on by itself, as soon as it is saved, to everyone with one of the designations or in one of the categories chosen there. It is separate from Send and its list (see below).
- A **status** is not a field type. It is a **drop down with "Use as status" switched on** (Studio > the field). Its options are the statuses; the first is where a record starts; some can be marked **final**.
- One **history** structure for the whole organisation keeps every event of every record, for ever.

## Rules

| | |
|---|---|
| Who can Send / Update status | The holder, or an administrator. Not while the record waits for approval. |
| A final status | Only an administrator can change a record that is in one. |
| Completion | Cannot go down (an administrator can). 0 to 100. |
| Comments, completion and a file | Optional on an Update status unless the form's status says otherwise: on the status dropdown (Studio, after "Use as status") switch on **Comments are required**, **Completion is required**, **Hours worked are required** and/or **A file is required** (`commentsRequired`, `completionRequired`, `hoursRequired`, `filesRequired` on the field). Then `record.status` refuses an update without them ("Add your comments.", "Enter the completion.", "Enter the hours worked.", "Attach a file."; hours of 0 count; a completion of 0 counts, spaces do not count as a comment, an empty list or someone else's file does not count as a file), administrators included, and the card's form marks them required. `record.actions` carries the four flags. |
| Hidden fields | A form with a status gets four hidden fields named after the status field (`stage` gives `stage_completion`, `stage_comments`, `stage_files`, `stage_hours`; types: whole number 0 to 100, text up to 1000 characters, files (several), number 0 to 24 with two decimals). They are made when the form is saved (a form saved before this gets them the next time it is saved, until then an update writes nothing extra) and each Update status writes the **latest value given** into them (an update that does not give hours leaves the hours as they were, like the completion). So reports, queries, formulas and other forms can use them like any field. People never see or type in them: a value sent for them when a record is made or edited is ignored. A field someone made with one of those names is never overwritten: the save is refused with the name, and a hidden field of the same kind is taken as the status's own. The status's name can be at most 29 characters (the longest part, `_completion`, must still fit the 40 of a field name) and the form at most 96 other fields. If the status is renamed or its switch turned off, the old four stay as ordinary hidden fields with their data (delete them in the builder if they are not needed). The history keeps every update (the `Hours` column), these keep only the latest. A **total over time** (all the hours of every update of a record, or of a person) is a calculation over the history: in the builder, a field that fills itself from the form *Record history*, **Total** of *Hours worked*, where *Record* is this record's number (administrators only, `docs/FETCH_FROM_CONNECTUM.md`, "Calculations"). |
| Hours worked | Every Update status can also say the hours worked (`hours`): optional unless the status says so (the **Hours worked are required** switch, off by default), a number from 0 to 24 with at most two decimals (a JSON number, or text such as `"2.5"`). Anything else is refused ("Hours worked must be a number from 0 to 24, with at most two decimals."). It is kept with the update in the history (`Hours` column of `record_history`, `hours` on the event) and on the record as the last hours given (`record.actions` answers it as `hours`, `null` when none). An update that only brings hours is an update even on the same status (like a completion). Histories made before this get the `Hours` column the next time one is written. |
| The status field | Locked in the form: it starts at the first option and changes through Update status only ("The status changes through Update status only."). One status per form, up to 20 options, no Multi Select, no options from a source. |
| Who a record can be sent to | Only on a form with **Enable send** on (`sendEnabled`); otherwise `record.send`, `record.targets` and `record.people` answer "Send is not switched on for this form." and the card has no Send. Per form, **send rules** (Studio, Send > "Who it can be sent to"; switching Send off removes them). Each rule: an optional condition on the form's own fields, and the designations, categories, people, the initiator and/or the assignor it allows. No rules: any active person. The rules that apply are added together. |
| Auto send | A form with `autoSend: true` and `autoSendRules: [{when?, designations, categories, users}]` (at least one rule; each names someone; the same shape and limits as send rules, 20 rules, 100 names each, without the two roles: a new record has no sender to go back to) sends each new record, after it is saved (or approved, for a form with approval), to every **active** person named by the rules whose `when` holds for the record, at most 50, **never the person who saved it**. A `when` is a condition on the form's own fields (`{field, op: "eq"\|"ne"\|"gt"\|"lt"\|"between"\|"notempty", value}`); on a multi select, "is X" holds when X is one of the values chosen and "is not X" when it is none of them (the same for send rules). **One person: it is theirs (they hold it). Several: it is shared with all of them equally** (`claimants` on the record; the stored holder stays the author, who may no longer act). Each of them has it in "With me", in their feed and in **My Work Space** (a card, with the record's menu), may open it, and joins the record's conversation; **whoever sends it on or updates its status first takes it**: they become the holder, the others lose it (off their lists, told with a live refresh) and may only open it. Two acting at once: one takes it, the other is refused (`forbidden`, or `stale` for a screen that saw an older state). An administrator may act too (and takes it). The history row of the taking says `claimed: true`. If one of the people it is shared with leaves, they drop out; with one left it is theirs, with none left it is back with the author. `record.actions` carries `claimants` (empty when it is held). One `sent` row goes into the history when it is shared (`via: "auto"`, `to` lists everyone, remarks "Sent automatically."). Auto send is **not held to the send rules** and works with Send off (then the people it was sent to can open it but have no Send; Update status only if the form has a status). No rule applies, or nobody active: the record stays with its author. Switching it off removes the rules; records already sent stay where they are. Best effort: a failure here never stops the record from being saved. In Studio both lists use one editor: Designations, Categories, People (`cfg.people`), "Send it conditionally" (the field's own values are offered for a drop down or multi select). **People chosen in the record itself** (a rule's `fields`, "From a field of the form"): whoever is chosen in a field that holds people when the record is made gets it. That is a drop down of Employees or People, **and** a Drop Down (or Multi Select) that reads a people field of another form (a project master's "Project team"), a Drop Down that reads one that reads one (through at most four other forms), and a Fill that copies such a field from the chosen record; a value that another form keeps by an alias or an employee code is turned into the person through THAT field's own Save. Not inside a grid section (a value per row) and not a status. Saving a rule that names any other field is refused ("An auto send rule can only take people from a field that holds people"); `query.forms` marks the fields that hold people (`person: true`) so Studio can offer them. |
| Two people at once | Every action carries `expected` (the status and holder the person saw). If someone got there first the answer is `stale` and nothing changes. `requestId` makes a retry harmless. |
| Someone who held it leaves (is de-activated) | It goes to their manager, else an administrator, else the initiator, and the history says so. |
| Who may open a record | As before, and everyone who has held it. They stay in the record's conversation. |

## Commands (WebSocket `sd` action only: there is no `POST /api/sd/<command>` route for these; a POST answers `not_found` "No such endpoint." (verified 16 Nov 2026, `docs/examples/smartview/`, file `http-post-api-sd-record-actions`))

| Command | Arguments | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| `record.actions` | `{id}` | `{items:[{id:"send"|"status", label, enabled, why?}], holder, claimants, initiator, assignor, status, statuses, final, finalStatuses, completion, minCompletion, hours, commentsRequired, completionRequired, hoursRequired, filesRequired, isAdmin, hasRules}`\|"status", label, enabled, why?}], holder, initiator, assignor, status, statuses, final, completion, commentsRequired, completionRequired, isAdmin, hasRules}`. `status`, `statuses` and `final` are empty for a form without a status. |
| `record.targets` | `{id}` | `{unrestricted, designations, categories, initiator, assignor, blocked}`: what Send may offer for this record now. |
| `record.people` | `{id, q?, designation?, category?}` | `{people:[{username, name, designation}], total}`: at most 50, by name; the sender and the holder are left out. |
| `record.send` | `{id, via, person?, value?, reason?, remarks?, files?:[fileId], expected?:{status, holder}, requestId?}` | `{id, holder, assignor, status, completion, files}`. `via`: `user`, `designation` (`value` = the designation), `category` (`value`), `initiator`, `assignor`. `person` for the first three. |
| `record.status` | `{id, status, comments?, percent?, hours?, files?, expected?, requestId?}` | `{id, holder, status, completion, hours, final, files}`; `completion` is what was stored (100 for a closing status). Completion rules: see **Completion and closing** below. |
| `records.mine` | `{as?: "with"|"raised"|"involved"|"team", limit?≤200, offset?}` | `{submissions, structures, total, hasMore, next}`: open records with me (default), raised by me, or **team** (a manager: the open records with the people who report to me, whoever raised them; empty for anyone else), or **involved** (records I was given or shared with, or acted on, whether or not I still hold them, never ones I made: My Workspace shows them as cards and they stay after I send them on or someone else takes them).\|"raised", limit?≤200, offset?}` | `{submissions, structures, total, hasMore, next}`: open records with me (default) or raised by me. Each submission has `holder`, `completion`, `by`; `structures[<form>].status = {field, statuses, final}` for a form with a status. |
| `records.summary` | `{ids:[1..50 record numbers]}` | `{summaries:[{id, tstruct, record:{status, holder, claimants, completion, final, ts, by}}], omitted}`: the state of many records in one call; records the caller may not read, and ones that do not exist, are left out alike. |
| `records.history` | `{structure?, record?, actor?, event?, limit?≤200, before?}` (administrator) | `{events, more}`, newest first; `before` is a row id. |
| `submission.history` | `{id}` | the events of one record, oldest first (as before, now from the organisation history), `names`, and `files: {<id>: {id, name, size, mime}}` for the files that came with an action (an event lists their ids in `files`). Whoever may open the record may read it and open those files (`GET /api/sd/files/<id>`): its author, everyone it was sent to or shared with, administrators. |

Errors: `forbidden` (not the holder), `invalid` (the record is in a final status, or waits for approval, or the request is wrong: the sentence says what), `stale`, `not_found` (not a person who may see the record), `rate_limited`.

Live events: `submissions_changed {id, tstruct, action, record}` to the holder, the previous holder, the initiator, the actor and everyone it was sent or shared to; `ticket_assigned {id, tstruct, action, by, record}` to the new holder (the notification opens the Inbox). The record's conversation (when the form has one) gets a card for each action.

**The `record` summary** (status, holder, claimants, completion, final, ts, by) is on both events, on the form card (`form.record`) and in the batch command `records.summary {ids: [up to 50]}` -> `{summaries:[{id, tstruct, record}], omitted}`. It is built by one function (`sd_actions:summary/2`) and equals what `record.actions` says to the same person; it is given only to a person who may read the record (the rule `record.actions` uses: `sd_actions:access/3`), so an app can redraw a row or a card without a call per record. A person who may not read the record gets the event or card as before, without `record`; in `records.summary` such a record and one that does not exist are left out alike (`omitted`). Details and shapes: `docs/CONNECTUM_API.md` ("The `record` summary").


## Completion and closing (the rules for a status update)

In simple words. They apply **only to a form whose status has at least one final (closing) option**; a form with no final status behaves as it always did (0 to 100, a member cannot lower it, an administrator only on a record that is final).

1. **Closing sets 100.** Moving to a final status always stores completion 100, whatever `percent` was sent (none, a lower number, or 100). It is never refused for that. The history event, the result, the record, the hidden `<status>_completion` field and the record summary all say 100. With several final statuses, any of them gives 100. The "Completion is required" switch is met by closing.
2. **100 is only for closing.** `percent: 100` on a status that does not close is refused: code `invalid`, message "100% completion is only for a closing status. Choose a closing status." The server never picks a final status for you. A status that does not close takes 0 to 99. Anything outside 0 to 100, or not a whole number, is still "Completion must be a number from 0 to 100." (also when closing).
3. **No `percent` keeps the completion** on a status that does not close.
4. **It does not go down** for a member ("Completion cannot go down."); an **administrator may lower it on any record** they can update (including a closed one they reopen).
5. **Old records** that are final but below 100 are left as stored; nothing is rewritten. Only a new update to a final status writes 100.
6. **A record at a status that does not close never holds 100.** When an administrator moves such a record (for example a closed one being reopened) with no `percent` and the stored completion is above 99, the server stores 99 (record, event, result, hidden field and summary all say 99). A member's update is never changed this way.
7. **An older record that already holds 100 on a status that does not close** (not a closed one): a member may send `percent` equal to the stored 100, as no change, and nothing else of 100; `minCompletion` is 100, so the control is fixed at that value. An administrator is held to rule 2.

The web dialog and the server share one table of cases (`tests/shared/status-rules-cases.json`). Editing a record (`submissions.update`) cannot change the status or the hidden `<status>_completion` field, and a Put API only sends a saved record out: neither skips these rules.

`record.actions` gives what a screen needs: `final` and `finalStatuses` (the same list: the final statuses **in the form's option order**; use the first one when a person asks for 100 on a status that does not close), `completion` (the current number, or `null`) and `minCompletion` (the lowest completion this person may send: the current one, or 0 when none; 0 for an administrator; for a form without final statuses it stays the current completion for an administrator too, unless the record is final). The `record` summary reads the same stored values.

**For the mobile team:** start the completion control at `completion` with `minCompletion` as its minimum. When the chosen status is in `finalStatuses`, show 100 (locked) and the warning "Closing this ticket sets completion to 100%. After it is closed, only an administrator can change it."; send nothing or 100, the server writes 100 either way. For a status that is not final cap the control at 99; if the person goes to 100, select the first `finalStatuses` entry instead (say so in a short note). When `minCompletion` is 100 on a status that does not close, fix the control at the current value and send no `percent`; an administrator reopening a record at 100 should send the 99 the control shows. Treat the server's errors above as the last word. A form with `finalStatuses: []` needs none of this.

## The history

A system structure `record_history` (read only, cannot be deleted; a Studio list shows it). One row per event: `op` (`created`, `edited` with before and after, `deleted`, `sent_for_approval`, `waiting`, `approved`, `declined`, `returned`, `resubmitted`, `approved_final`, `sent`, `status`, `reassigned`), `ts`, `by`, `to` (who was notified or sent to), `fromStatus`, `toStatus`, `completion`, `reason`, `remarks`, `structure`, `record`. Rows are never edited or removed. They go through the normal registry pipeline to PostgreSQL like any record.

## For the mobile team: a clean break

Removed: the `status` field type, `status.update`, `status.mine`, `status.get`, `status.history`, the `ticket` flag and status table on forms, the support pack, the form `status_<name>`. Use `record.actions` (draw the menu from `items`), `record.send`, `record.status`, `records.mine`, `submission.history`. A form message carries no status table, but now carries the small `record` summary (current status, holder, final) for people who may read the record; read `record.actions {id}` when the card's menu opens, and use `record` on the card, on `submissions_changed` and in `records.summary` to keep rows up to date.
