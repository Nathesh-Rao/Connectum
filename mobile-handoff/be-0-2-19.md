Session: WS `session_expired` (any reason, incl. `password_changed`) and `session_ended` end the session, stop the reconnect loop and go to Sign In; `connectNow` refuses an ended state until `start` after a new sign-in. 401 on a session call already signs out (a 401 `otp_invalid` / `invalid_credentials` no longer does).

6. Live summary: `RecordSummary`, `TicketRepository.applySummary` (ts order), `requestSummaries` (chunks of 50, 500 ms apart, no duplicate in flight), unknown record shows a dash.
7. Demo: Auto Fill date field on the demo ticket form; record 44 (admin, closed) and finalStatuses/minCompletion in `SeedTickets`.
