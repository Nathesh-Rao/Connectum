# Fonts

Files here are the ones the app bundles (`pubspec.yaml`): Plus Jakarta Sans 400, 500, 600, 700, 800 (the UI font) and Space Grotesk 600, 700 (display brand text).

**Licence files were not found in the app repository.** `CLAUDE.md` states that Space Grotesk is bundled under the SIL Open Font License (OFL) with source fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk. Plus Jakarta Sans is distributed under the same licence on Google Fonts (fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans) *(inferred: the repo records no licence for it)*. Before the web team ships these files, take the OFL text and the copyright lines from those Google Fonts pages (or each font's `OFL.txt` in its upstream repository) and put them next to the fonts; this guide does not invent them.
